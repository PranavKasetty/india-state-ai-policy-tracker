          About the data

          What counts

          An action on AI or robotics by a state government, or by the central government in a specific state: announcing, funding, signing, deploying, regulating, training or hosting. Private-sector news with no government involved, crime stories, opinion pieces and social media posts are left out.

          How it's gathered

          Daily Google News searches for every state since ChatGPT's launch on 30 November 2022: in English, plus Hindi for the nine Hindi-speaking states and Marathi for Maharashtra (translated, with the original shown). A language model checks each headline against a written rubric, assigns its types, sector and stage, and merges reports of the same development.

          Known gaps

          Only headlines are read, not full articles. Other regional languages are not covered, so states read in Hindi or Marathi show more local activity. Counts measure news coverage, not activity itself: AI news has grown a lot since 2022, so rising monthly counts partly reflect more reporting. Some entries will be misclassified. AI summaries cite their sources; check them before quoting.
