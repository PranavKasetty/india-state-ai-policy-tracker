Manipur has integrated artificial intelligence across governance, education, legal proceedings, and conservation efforts since late 2022. Significant actions include the Manipur High Court using ChatGPT for legal research and delivering a landmark verdict [1, 2, 3], and partnerships with Microsoft and the BHASHINI Division to advance multilingual AI and digital governance [1, 2]. The state has also promoted AI in classrooms and academic research, launched an AI career guidance hub, and explored AI applications for wildlife trafficking and tribal development [1, 2, 3, 4]. Additionally, initiatives span digital tourism, journalism workshops, and language translation tools like the app 'Ishaan' [1, 2, 3].
