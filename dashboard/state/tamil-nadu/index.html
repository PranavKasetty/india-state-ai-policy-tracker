Tamil Nadu has driven extensive artificial intelligence adoption through infrastructure developments, public service automation, and targeted educational initiatives. Key infrastructure projects include partnering to establish a Sovereign AI Park, inaugurating South India's largest AI-ready data center campus, and collaborating with IIT Madras to create an AI Data Centre and state-owned GPU cluster [1, 2, 3, 4]. The state has enhanced public service delivery by deploying WhatsApp chatbots like 'Namma Arasu' for government services and launching AI systems for wildlife conservation, healthcare appointment booking, and policing [1, 2, 3, 4]. Furthermore, Tamil Nadu has integrated AI into its education system by training government school students and teachers, launching specialized AI learning platforms, and introducing AI trades in vocational training institutes [1, 2, 3, 4].
