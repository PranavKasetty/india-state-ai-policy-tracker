Sikkim's AI policy activity spans multiple sectors, notably featuring transport modernization through an AI-driven Traffic Management System and related e-challan systems, which have also prompted public SOP adjustments [1, 2, 3]. Educational and administrative initiatives include launching foundational learning programmes in government schools, collaborating with Google for higher education, and partnering with NIELIT Gangtok to establish an AI Centre of Excellence [1, 2, 3]. Additionally, the state has explored artificial intelligence applications for boosting tourism via permit systems, supporting agriculture, disaster prediction, and workforce empowerment [1, 2, 3, 4, 5].
