Nagaland has actively advanced its artificial intelligence initiatives across governance, agriculture, and infrastructure since late 2025. Significant actions include the inauguration of an AI Centre of Excellence for Skilling by Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman, the unveiling of the Eliona AI Supercomputer for disaster preparedness, and the launch of an AI-powered unified digital agriculture ecosystem [1, 2, 3]. The state has also focused heavily on education, capacity building, and digital governance through faculty development workshops, hackathons like "Agentic AI Saksham," and school education reform plans [1, 2, 3, 4]. Additionally, administrative reach has been expanded through initiatives such as the NSEDM WhatsApp chatbot for skill development services and academic seminars on AI and green sustenance [1, 2].
